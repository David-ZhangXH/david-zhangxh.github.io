david.world — concept board 02

Three worlds, one site: who hosts whom?

You picked the Observatory, the Village, and the Desk. They can all live in one site — the question is which world is the front door. Three architectures:

RECOMMENDED

CThe Desk Portal — “everything I make starts here”

You arrive at a cinematic desk at midnight. Three objects glow — and each one is a door.

Why it wins: all three of your picks get equal billing, tied together by a true story — a statistician's work really does live behind a screen on a desk. Effort curve: ships in stages. Stage 1 = the desk alone (already a wow). Stage 2 = the galaxy. Stage 3 = the village. Something impressive is live early. Signature transitions: the camera diving through the monitor glass; the handheld boot-up jingle before the village fades in.

AThe Observatory Hub — “fly first”

You spawn in open space. Papers are stars you read in place; a few special planets are worlds you can land on.

Feel: grand, cosmic, exploratory — the biggest “whoa” on first load. Trade-off: the heavy 3D (flight controls, camera, performance) must be built first, before anything ships. Landings come later.

BThe Village Hub — “play first”

You spawn as pixel-David in the town square. Every building is a section; two buildings hide the other worlds.

Feel: the most charming and playable; best on phones; lightest to build overall. Trade-off: everything inherits the pixel art style — the galaxy becomes a cute 2D starfield rather than a vast 3D one. Less “fancy”, more “lovable”.
